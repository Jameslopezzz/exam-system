Examination Guidelines

Academic integrity policy for online assessments

What is monitored

    Violation policy

    1. Each detected incident is recorded with a timestamp and shown to your professor in real time.
    2. You receive an on-screen alert for every recorded violation.
    3. Reaching 3 violations submits your examination automatically and locks all answers.
    4. Copy, paste, right-click and text selection are disabled inside the examination page.
    5. Leaving the examination window is also recorded.

    Accessibility

    Every question can be read aloud using the built-in text-to-speech control. Reading speed can be adjusted, and reading stops automatically when you move to another item.

    System requirements

    • A working webcam with camera permission granted.
    • Updated Chrome, Edge or Firefox browser.
    • Stable internet connection for the full duration.
    • Well-lit room with only the examinee present.

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens if my camera disconnects mid-exam?

    The proctoring panel shows a "No camera" notice. Restore the camera and refresh immediately; time keeps running and prolonged absence is reported to your professor.

    Does switching tabs really count as a violation?

    Yes. Leaving the examination window — another tab, another app, or minimising the browser — is recorded with a timestamp.

    Can I still type my essay if pasting is blocked?

    Typing is fully allowed. Only copy, cut and paste are blocked, and each attempt is logged as a violation.

    What happens after the third violation?

    Your examination is submitted automatically, all answers are locked, and the violation report is sent to your professor together with your answers.

    When are results released?

    Scores appear on the Results page once your professor releases them. Essay items are checked manually, so they may take longer.
